# Evaluation Metrics

  - Pre-simulation Paper: guarantee that all individuals for all teams complete this aspect of the simulation so they are completely aware of their roles and responsibilities; if not all duties/responsibilities are not listed they will be penalized; thorough and accurate details will be integral towards receiving a proper grade
  - Pre-simulation Presentation: ensures that all individuals are aware of everyone’s roles and responsibilities; creates a public speaking environment that will be necessary for those involved in high-stake positions so that communication will be effective 
  - Red Team: a red team can analyze how well the key stakeholders participated in mitigating and responding to the threats; ensure that they are informing teams or individuals working on an incident that information needs to be reevaluated and resubmitted; required to update all teams via announcement that an incident has been resolved or when a new incident arises 
  - Ranking Risks: the ranking of risks would help determine the highest priorities to be tackled first to ensure we utilize our resources efficiently 
  - Crisis Response: we can analyze the time it takes to respond to an incident, deploy necessary actors, and justify actions and proper policies 
  - Communication: ensure that all teams work collaboratively and efficiently. Examine that local, state, and federal agencies are able to seamlessly bring together their skill sets and knowledge regarding their respective roles to control and mitigate attacks and issues that arise
  - Post-simulation Paper: team members and individuals are to self-evaluate participation, communication, and collaboration to ensure that tasks were carried out efficiently and effectively; guarantees that team members and individuals are held accountable if tasks were not completed
