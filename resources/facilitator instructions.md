# Facilitator Instructions

  - Pre-Simulation: The facilitator of this simulation will need to give each participant a position that is involved in the simulation. There are around 28 positions available for students (some are the same position) to be able to do. It is up to the facilitator how they would like to go about delegating positions. Once students are assigned positions they will research their position’s responsibilities and resources. The facilitator will then separate the participants into four groups: the red team, federal government, local government, and individual actors. Allow the participants time to introduce themselves and their roles to their groups and begin discussion. 

  - During the Simulation: This is an event-based simulation that is focusing primarily in central DC. To begin the simulation the first 4 events should be released (as suggested below) for students to be able to address. All events should be addressed by everyone that is involved in that event. After each event is answered there should be another released event until they get to the final event. Throughout the simulation the facilitator should ensure that everyone is actively participating and seeing collaboration within groups and between groups.
    
  - Along with releasing the events the facilitator should also aid the Red Team in reading responses and determining whether they are acceptable responses or not. Throughout the simulation the facilitator should be watching the student’s engagement and be there for any questions, comments, or concerns that the participants may face. To end the simulation all events should be responded to and completed.
