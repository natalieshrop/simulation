Throughout the simulation the facilitator should ensure that everyone is actively participating and seeing collaboration within groups and between groups.

 - The smaller terrorist attacks are committed by individuals influenced by right-wing (anti-government) terrorist groups in the D.C. area. They have not caused any deaths, only injuries and infrastructure damage.
