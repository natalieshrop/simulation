# First Scenarios

Main Events:
 - An anonymous tip about there being an individual potentially committing espionage within the Department of Homeland Security
 - There was someone attempting to break into the firewall protecting sensitive data in the Department of Treasury

Smaller Terrorist Attacks:
 - Attack 1: 2 men were arrested after being seen carrying firearms walking into a Mosque, where they shot and injured 3 individuals.
 - Attack 2:  A 17 year old boy used a self made explosive at Lincoln Park but only injured himself as it misfired. It was later discovered that he was influenced by individuals he met in online chat rooms discussing anti-government, xenophobic, white and male supremacy topics
